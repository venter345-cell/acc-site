How billing works

  • All prices are in South African rand (ZAR). ACC is not VAT registered, so no VAT is charged.
  • Billed in advance. The setup fee and the first 4 week fee are paid before work starts. After that, the 4 week fee is billed in advance at the start of each 4 week cycle.
  • Secure payments through Paystack. You can pay by card or other methods Paystack offers. ACC never sees or stores your full card details.
  • Ad spend is separate. If we run ads, you pay the ad budget directly to the ad platforms (for example Meta or Google). It is not part of ACC's fees.
  • Cancel any time after the first 4 weeks with 30 days written notice. See our Refund and Cancellation Policy.

Not included

  • Ad spend paid to ad platforms
  • Full website redesigns and custom software
  • Sales closing on your behalf
  • Guarantees of leads, bookings, sales or revenue. We guarantee what we control: delivery dates, setup done as agreed and a weekly report.

Our delivery guarantee

If we receive the access and approvals we need within 48 hours, your system goes live within 10 business days. If we miss that date, your next 4 week fee is waived.

Work outside the scope above is quoted in writing before we start.
