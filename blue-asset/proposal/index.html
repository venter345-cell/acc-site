What we found

Six things that cost you bookings today, and are easy to fix.

We looked only at what anyone can see in public, on 3 November 2026. These are the gaps between your team's effort and booked consultations.

01

Careful investors may not book after they search your name

A careful investor will Google you before they book. Right now the search results do not tell your full story. Your site does not yet show your team, your 4.8 star Google reviews or your press, and there is little fresh, helpful content with your name on it.

What you get: a page that shows your team, reviews, press and FSCA licence, plus a steady flow of helpful articles, so good, current content is what people find.
02

Your Google profile does not help people book

You have 4.8 stars from 13 reviews, which is a great start. But the profile looks unclaimed, it has no phone number, no team or office photos, no updates, no booking link and a general category. The newest reviews are about 7 months old, with no replies.

What you get: a claimed and complete Google profile with your phone number, photos, booking link, the right category, regular updates, replies to reviews and a steady flow of new reviews.
03

You cannot tell which posts and ads bring bookings

Your website and ads count visits, but not enquiries or booked consultations. So there is no clear line from a post or ad to a booking, and no way to know what to spend more on.

What you get: tracking on your website and ads that follows each person from first click to booked consultation, in one simple weekly report.
04

People searching for what you offer may not find you

Google sees every page on your site as the same page, because they all share one title and description. That makes it hard to show up when someone searches for a retirement income plan.

What you get: each key page clearly labelled, so Google can tell your pages apart, plus helpful articles that answer the questions your clients ask.
05

Your social posts are not bringing in bookings

Posting has slowed since August, and posts do not yet lead to bookings. Most posts are general motivational graphics with a handful of likes. They do not yet show your team, your IS21 launch or a clear way to book.

What you get: regular posts with real people, IS21 news and useful education, made from your website articles, each one linking to a booking.
06

Warm interest goes cold before it becomes a booking

Today a prospect can WhatsApp, call or fill in a form. There is no booking calendar and no automatic follow up, so warm people can go cold before an adviser reaches them.

What you get: prospects book straight into an adviser's diary, with SMS, WhatsApp and email reminders and follow up handled for you.
