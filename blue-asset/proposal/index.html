What we found

Five things that are easy to fix and worth fixing first.

We looked only at what anyone can see in public. Your team already does a lot. These are the gaps between that effort and booked consultations.

01

What people find when they search your name

A careful investor will Google you before they book. Right now the search results do not tell your full story. Your site does not yet show your team, your 4.8 star Google reviews or your press.

Fix: a trust page with your team, reviews, press and FSCA licence, plus a steady flow of new reviews.
02

You cannot see which posts and ads create bookings

The Meta Pixel counts page views only, and the Google Tag Manager container on the site is set up but empty. So there is no clear line from a click to a booked consultation.

Fix: GTM, GA4 and Meta lead and booking events, with one simple weekly report.
03

Google sees every page as the same page

Every page on the site shares one title and description, and there is no sitemap. That makes it hard to show up for searches like retirement income plan South Africa.

Fix: page titles, descriptions and a sitemap, or focused landing pages.
04

Lots of posts, not yet much reach

Your team posts often, which is great. The next step is a system behind the posts: retargeting, a clear offer and follow up for the people who show interest.

Fix: signed off creatives tested every week, linked to a booking page.
05

Interest does not yet flow into a booked time

Today a prospect can WhatsApp, call or fill in a form. There is no booking calendar and no automatic follow up, so warm people can go cold before an adviser reaches them.

Fix: a consultation calendar linked to adviser diaries, with SMS, WhatsApp and email reminders and follow up.
