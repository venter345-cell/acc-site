How we collect, use and protect your personal information under POPIA.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains how Ads Creation Centre (ACC) collects and uses personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

2. What we collect

  • Contact details you give us when you call, WhatsApp, email, book a call or fill in a form: name, business name, email, phone number and your message.
  • Client and billing details: business details, invoice details and payment records. Card payments are processed by Paystack. ACC does not see or store your full card details.
  • Website usage data: pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location, how you arrived and how you use the site. We collect this with Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity, using cookies and similar technologies. Clarity may record anonymised session replays and heatmaps.
  • CRM records: we keep contact and conversation records in a CRM such as GoHighLevel.
  • Business contact research: for business to business outreach we may use publicly available business contact details, such as a name, job title and business email or LinkedIn profile.

3. Why we use it (purpose)

  • To reply to you and book calls.
  • To deliver, manage and bill our services.
  • To improve our website and measure our marketing.
  • To send relevant business communication, with an easy way to opt out.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting duties.

4. Legal basis

We process personal information only where POPIA allows it, for example:

  • your consent (you can withdraw it at any time);
  • to conclude or perform a contract with you;
  • to comply with the law;
  • our legitimate interests, or yours, such as responding to business enquiries and limited business to business outreach, where this does not override your rights.

For direct marketing by email or SMS we follow section 69 of POPIA. Every message includes a way to opt out.

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, under agreements that require them to protect it:

6. Cross border transfers

Some of these providers store data outside South Africa, for example in the United States or the European Union. We only use providers that are subject to laws, binding rules or agreements that give a level of protection similar to POPIA, as section 72 of POPIA requires.

7. Cookies

Our website uses cookies for analytics and to understand how the site is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works if you do.

8. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become clients: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Client and billing records: as long as needed for the work, and then for as long as tax and company law requires (generally 5 years).
  • Analytics data: according to the retention settings of the analytics tools (for example 14 months in Google Analytics).

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including access controls, two factor authentication where available and reputable providers. If there is a security compromise that affects you, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

10. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • ask whether we hold your personal information and get a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to us processing it, including for direct marketing;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

To use these rights, email our Information Officer at Ruben@acreationc.com. We reply within 30 days.
