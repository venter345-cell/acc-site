2. When work starts

Work starts at whichever comes first:

  • the kickoff call, or
  • when we begin setup work after your payment (for example research, account setup or building pages), which we confirm to you by email.

If you cancel in writing after paying but before work starts, we refund the setup fee and first 4 week fee in full.

3. Setup fee

The setup fee pays for the time and tools we commit to building your system. Once work has started, the setup fee is non refundable.

4. Recurring 4 week fee and cancellation

  • The first 4 weeks are a fixed commitment.
  • After that there is no lock in. You can cancel any time with 30 days written notice, sent by email to Ruben@acreationc.com.
  • We confirm your cancellation in writing within 2 business days.
  • Your plan stays active, and is billed, until the end of the 4 week cycle in which your 30 day notice period ends. After that, no further charges are made.
  • We do not give partial refunds for unused days in a 4 week cycle that has started.

5. Our delivery guarantee

If we receive the access and approvals we need within 48 hours, your system goes live within 10 business days. If we miss that date, your next 4 week fee is waived. This is applied as a waived fee on your next cycle, not a cash refund. If you cancel instead, we refund that next 4 week fee if it has already been charged.

6. Ad spend

Ad budgets are paid by you directly to platforms such as Meta, Google or LinkedIn. ACC never receives this money, so ACC cannot refund it. Any credit or refund of ad spend is between you and the platform.

7. How to request a refund

  1. Email Ruben@acreationc.com with the subject "Refund request".
  2. Include your business name, the payment date, the amount and the reason.
  3. We reply within 2 business days and give you a decision within 10 business days.
  4. Approved refunds are paid to your original payment method through Paystack. How long it takes to show in your account depends on Paystack and your bank.

8. Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you may have under the Consumer Protection Act or other South African law where it applies to you.
